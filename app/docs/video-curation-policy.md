# Video curation policy

Version: `2026-11-07.v1`  
Owner: Caroline  
Status: editorial policy approved in the cleanup chat; not yet implemented in production.

## Purpose and categories

Encore Atlas prioritizes meaningful music footage and context about the featured artist. Classify the whole video by its main purpose. Each YouTube video belongs in at most one section per artist.

| Public label | Existing storage value | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live Performances | `concert` | Concerts, festivals, Tiny Desk, KEXP, Audiotree, filmed studio sessions, and musical performances on SNL/Fallon or other television shows. |
| Music Videos | `music_video` | Official narrative, conceptual, animated, and other music videos with meaningful visual content. |
| Interviews & Films | `interview` | Artist conversations, documentaries, making-of films, tour documentaries, and third-party video essays about the artist. |

These are proposed public labels; retain the existing storage values. The earlier “Sessions & Videos” category was superseded during the interview. A live performance does not become a music video because its title says “official video.”

Full performance sessions with interview breaks belong in Live Performances. Separately uploaded interviews belong in Interviews & Films. A documentary containing concert excerpts remains in Interviews & Films when telling the story is its main purpose.

## Eligibility

- The artist must perform, participate, or be the subject. Include collaborations involving the artist. Exclude unrelated artists with similar names and other musicians covering the artist without their involvement. Removal is scoped to the artist's page, not every occurrence of the YouTube ID.
- Exclude audio over a still image, static full-album uploads, concert audio without performance footage, and text-only lyric videos. Animation or narrative footage remains eligible even when it contains lyrics. Duration alone is not grounds for exclusion.
- Include complete individual songs, including short songs. Exclude promotional snippets, teasers, and incomplete performance fragments. Short interviews may qualify when they contain a complete, worthwhile exchange.
- Fan footage is eligible when the performance is watchable and audible. Rare material may have rough production. Exclude footage dominated by distortion, obstructed views, or crowd noise. Verify audiovisual quality by viewing/listening, not title alone.

## Ranking and diversity

- Generally favor strong full concerts and substantial sessions over one-song TV appearances; exceptional individual performances may still stand out.
- Include complete individual songs and limit repeated clips from the same show so a split upload does not dominate an artist's results.
- Keep a single Live Performances section and the existing decade filter. Do not introduce visible full-show/song filters.
- Numeric ranking weights, same-show caps, and session-length thresholds are not agreed yet. Do not present guessed values as editorial requirements.

## Human approval and manual picks

- Existing manual picks are eligible for a one-time audit against this policy. Prior approval does not exempt them, but it also does not authorize blanket automatic deletion. Propose and explicitly approve individual changes.
- Picks approved under this policy are protected against future automatic removal or recategorization. A later explicit human decision may override them.
- Record policy version and approval provenance. The current `is_manually_added` boolean cannot distinguish legacy picks from approvals under this policy. Do not infer the distinction from video upload dates or silently clear existing protection flags.
- Approved removals should remain excluded from future automatic discovery for that artist unless explicitly reversed.

## Calibration and uncertainty

Begin with a representative batch of existing videos. Show thumbnail, watch link, current placements, proposed keep/move/remove decision, destination, evidence, uncertainty, and a concise reason. Permit batch approval with corrections to exceptions.

Jev is intended to recommend decisions after integration; the initial examples were proposed by Codex and approved by Caroline, not generated by Jev. Test agreement on fresh cases as well as the approved examples before enabling automatic decisions. No accuracy threshold or autonomous rollout has been approved yet.

During calibration, keep uncertain existing content unchanged and collect uncertain or consequential decisions (especially removals) into manageable batches. Holding uncertain new finds out of public results is the proposed default, pending validation of review volume; do not require routine per-artist admin visits as the long-term workflow.
